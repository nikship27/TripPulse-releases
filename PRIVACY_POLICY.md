# Privacy Policy for TripPulse AI

**Effective Date:** August 29, 2026  
**App Name:** TripPulse AI - Intelligent Travel Concierge  
**Developer:** TripPulse Engineering Team  
**Contact:** support@trippulse.app

---

### 1. Overview
TripPulse AI ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the TripPulse mobile application.

### 2. Information We Process
- **Travel Preferences:** Search criteria such as travel vibe, budget limits, trip duration, pace preferences, and dietary restrictions are processed locally or sent securely over encrypted HTTPS connections to generate personalized itineraries.
- **Offline Storage:** Your saved trips and custom itineraries are stored locally on your device in secure sandbox storage (`SharedPreferences` / SQLite).
- **No Personal Identity Tracking:** We do not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, or biometric data to use the travel planner.

### 3. Permissions Used
- **Internet Permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Used strictly to query live destination places, hotel ratings, and multi-agent reasoning endpoints.
- **Network State (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Used to detect offline state and switch automatically to on-device planning mode.

### 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties
- We **never** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
- External links (e.g. hotel booking, flight links) redirect to official third-party providers (Google Flights, Booking.com) under their respective privacy policies.

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion
- All cached search data and saved itineraries can be permanently cleared at any time directly within the app: **Settings > Data & Storage > Clear Search Cache**.

### 6. Children's Privacy
TripPulse AI does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
